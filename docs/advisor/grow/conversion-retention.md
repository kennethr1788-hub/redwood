# Grow conversion and retention

TOPIC: grow.conversion-retention
PURPOSE: Improve message match and the next useful customer action without sending or publishing.
WHEN TO USE: Landing-page CRO, forms/CTA, email, organic social, retention, creative preflight or local export.
REQUIRED FACTS: Audience and intended action; actual page/content observations; offer/proof; device/task; permission basis where relevant; available metrics; rights and capacity.

STABLE PRINCIPLES
Match the promise that brought the visitor to the destination and the actual outcome. Give the primary action an understandable label. Keep proof and trust signals truthful. Collect only information the task needs; use labels, accessible errors and appropriate recovery. A shorter form or attractive creative is a hypothesis for improvement, not a promised conversion uplift.

DECISION TREE
If visitors arrive but do not take the intended action, first check measurement, message match, clarity, proof, friction and technical failure. Inspect the actual mobile/keyboard journey and confirmation, not merely the hero section. Propose one change and a meaningful acceptance check. Keep visual polish separate from repairing a broken submission or misleading claim.

For email, clarify recipient expectations, permission basis, jurisdiction and suppression/unsubscribe process before any send recommendation. No contact list is needed in Advisor. Draft one purpose and useful next step; confirm sender-system and current legal/provider prerequisites separately. Opens may not represent human reading; examine downstream behavior and complaints with scope. No universal send cadence, inbox guarantee or compliance certificate follows from a brief.

For organic social, choose a sustainable purpose and format suited to the audience and actual capacity. Paid creative guidance is not automatically an organic ranking rule. Drafts, a calendar and exported bundles remain local artifacts; Grow does not become an automatic sender, poster or scheduler. EXPORTED != PUBLISHED. Preflight/readability checks do not prove platform acceptance or commercial effectiveness.

COMMON FAILURE MODES
Fake testimonials or scarcity, clickbait that the page cannot fulfill, purchased lists treated as consent, metrics without denominators, excessive posting before understanding the constraint, or a local export called a live campaign.

STOP CONDITIONS: Unverified claim/rights, missing permission or suppression, unclear sender jurisdiction, broken destination or external delivery uncertainty. Stop the affected action and identify the needed evidence; do not repeat an uncertain send or publication.
WHAT NOT TO CLAIM: Guaranteed conversion/revenue, global legal approval, inbox placement, publication or a verified user journey from a screenshot alone.
NEXT ACTION / OUTPUT SHAPE: Intended outcome; observed friction or hypothesis; one content/form/CTA change; preserve list; review/measurement and stop condition.
