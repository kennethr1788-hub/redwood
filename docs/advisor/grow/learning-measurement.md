# Grow learning and measurement

TOPIC: grow.learning-measurement
PURPOSE: Diagnose the constrained stage before recommending more activity or budget.
WHEN TO USE: CTR/conversion changes, creative testing, budget increases, attribution or inconclusive results.
REQUIRED FACTS: Source/report and revision; metric definitions and denominators; complete comparable windows/cohorts; currency/timezone; coverage; conversion lag; goal and guardrails.

STABLE PRINCIPLES
MISSING != ZERO. ASSOCIATION != CAUSATION. Planned, observed, attributed and incremental outcomes are different. Compare compatible populations and definitions before calculating a persuasive ratio. Aggregate compatible numerators and denominators rather than averaging percentages. Missing or zero denominators make many ratios unknown or undefined, not automatically favorable.

DECISION TREE
Are values present and meaningful? Are source, units, window, cohort, currency, timezone and coverage comparable? If not, name the gap and stop comparison. If yes, trace the journey from reach to response, qualified action and retained value. Find the constrained stage, consider tracking failure, audience mix, seasonality, lag and chance, then propose one useful test with a primary metric and guardrails.

If CTR rises while conversions stay flat, that may reflect different traffic quality, destination friction, lag or measurement changes. It does not by itself prove better demand or justify more spend. Before increasing budget, examine qualified outcomes, affordability, capacity and attribution limits. A small sample can support a practical observation without supporting a decisive causal claim.

When isolating a cause, change one material variable where practical; a whole-concept test supports concept-level conclusions only. Define the outcome and stopping/review condition before observing results. Keep the useful invariant fixed. Do not repeatedly peek, rerun or alter several variables until a favorable number appears.

CTR uses a specified click definition over impressions. A conversion rate needs a declared eligible denominator. Attributed revenue/spend is observed ROAS, not profit or incremental return. Revenue basis, refunds and costs matter. Cross-platform credit can overlap; reach is not generally additive. Current Grow comparison limits and INCONCLUSIVE outcomes remain authoritative; never relabel a timezone to make reports appear compatible.

COMMON FAILURE MODES
Scaling traffic into a broken funnel, averages of unequal rates, overlapping exports double-counted, ambiguous exported zero treated as observed absence, or a before/after trend called causal proof.

STOP CONDITIONS: Missing definition/denominator, incompatible reports, stale baseline, unknown exposure or a causal question unsupported by the design. Preserve uncertainty instead of inventing a winner.
WHAT NOT TO CLAIM: Guaranteed revenue/ROAS, tracking completeness, statistical certainty or authority to change account budgets.
NEXT ACTION / OUTPUT SHAPE: Comparable facts; constrained stage/hypotheses; one test; metric/guardrail; stop condition; evidence that would change the recommendation.
