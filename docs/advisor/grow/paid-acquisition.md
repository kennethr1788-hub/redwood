# Grow paid acquisition planning

TOPIC: grow.paid-acquisition
PURPOSE: Prepare a reviewable Meta/Google plan without taking account actions or spending.
WHEN TO USE: Channel choice, a small budget, Meta objective planning, Search versus Performance Max or creative/placement prerequisites.
REQUIRED FACTS: Business goal and event definition; audience/offer/destination; total affordable envelope and dates; measurement evidence; asset rights; current account/region constraints.

STABLE PRINCIPLES
Choose the measurable business outcome before a channel, objective or bid setting. Clicks are not qualified leads or sales. A budget plan is not spending permission. With a small envelope, avoid unnecessary fragmentation; useful learning is possible without promising statistically decisive results. Preserve a reserve and explicit stop/review criteria as proposals, not automatic controls.

DECISION TREE
For Meta, align goal → available objective → conversion location → performance event → measurement evidence → suitable creative/placement → bounded test. Missing event measurement calls for a measurement brief before conversion optimization. Detailed controls, eligibility, attribution and billing require current official/account confirmation. Research verified public outlines and limited excerpts, not the complete live account menu or numeric learning rules.

For Google, identify relevant search intent and a credible destination first. Search can test coherent query intent; exact matching is not simply literal text matching. Consider Performance Max separately when multi-inventory reach, useful assets, trustworthy goals and actual account controls fit. It is not the automatic choice for a small test. Confirm selected conversion goals, query exclusions and destination expansion rather than assuming defaults are appropriate.

A daily budget setting is not necessarily a hard total-envelope cap. Ask for actual billing/overdelivery rules before an execution-specific recommendation. Separate audience suggestions/signals from verified restrictions. Review important text, proof and CTA against each placement's current overlays; a correct aspect ratio does not prove readability or platform acceptance.

COMMON FAILURE MODES
Optimizing visits while claiming lead quality; duplicate conversion events; many tiny campaigns; unsupported learning thresholds; changes to audience, offer and creative called a single-variable test; stale bid names; imported plan treated as an active campaign.

STOP CONDITIONS: Missing rights or measurement, unknown exposure, unresolved policy/account eligibility, misleading claims or stale creative. Advisor may prepare a plan/handoff; it never submits ads, changes settings or spends.
WHAT NOT TO CLAIM: Guaranteed CPA/ROAS, account approval, an optimal budget, current dropdown values or Grow-native bidding controls.
NEXT ACTION / OUTPUT SHAPE: Goal; candidate channel and prerequisites; measurement gap; one creative variable; envelope for human review; stop/recheck evidence.
